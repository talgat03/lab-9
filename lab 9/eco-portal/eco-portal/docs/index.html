Eco-Tips

  • Rinse before sorting

    A quick rinse keeps glass and plastic clean enough to be recycled.

  • Flatten and stack

    Flat paper and cardboard take less space in the bin and on the truck.

  • Carry a bag

    A reusable bag replaces hundreds of single-use bags over a lifetime.
